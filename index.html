Design

Get to know
Clicklights.

3D view

  • Cap colors. The cap is the lens over the LEDs, the only part that shows through the channel. Choose Clear for a bright point of light, or Grey for a softer look that blends in during the day.

  • Channels. Clicklights sit inside a channel that mounts along the roofline, so only the cap shows. Pick the finish that matches your trim.

  • Ice and heat. The housing is sealed around the board and the lens, so the light keeps working through frozen nights and hot summer afternoons.

  • Rain and snow. Water and snow stay outside the sealed housing, so the lights stay up all year with no covers and no seasonal takedown.

  • Outward or downward. Mount Clicklights facing out on the fascia to put color on the roofline, or facing down from the soffit to wash light over the walls.

  • Control from the app. Turn the lights on, change colors and set schedules from your phone. Try the phone: switch the power, pick a color, or move the time slider to watch the sunset schedule.

  • Patterns. Start with pre-made patterns for holidays and game days, or build your own with any colors.

  • Light colors. Each Clicklight mixes RGB LEDs for color with dedicated warm white LEDs for everyday light. The color you pick stays on as you explore.
